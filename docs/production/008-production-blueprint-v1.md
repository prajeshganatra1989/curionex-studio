**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Official · Companion to Editorial Bible v1.0 & Voice & Narration Bible v1.0

**Format:** Vertical Shorts · **55–60 seconds** · **1080×1920** (9:16) unless a series brief says otherwise

> Every Curionex video should feel inevitable:  
> calm picture, clear idea, earned wonder—never noise.

## 1. Production Philosophy

### Brand picture

| Principle | In practice |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Educational** | Every shot teaches or supports the spoken idea |
| **Premium** | Clean grades, intentional framing, no junk overlays |
| **Minimal** | One focal idea on screen at a time |
| **Elegant** | Restraint as craft |
| **Modern** | Contemporary motion language—not trendy clutter |
| **Timeless** | Avoid meme UI, dated effects, platform gimmicks |
| **Curiosity-first** | Visuals open questions; they don’t spoil with shock |
| **Evidence-first** | Labels match Editorial evidence layers |
| **Wonder over spectacle** | Awe from clarity, not chaos |

### Never

| Never | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Noisy | Competes with VO; breaks trust |
| Cluttered | Cognitive overload in 9:16 |
| Trailer montage energy | Brand break (see Voice Bible) |
| Fake “glitch science” | Undermines evidence-first |
| Wall of text | Shorts are heard first, read second |
| Continuous whoosh every cut | Amateur signal |

> Pull quote: *BBC Earth patience. Apple clarity. Pixar intentionality—without cartoon chaos.*

### Mute-picture brand test

Watch once **without audio**.  
A stranger should still feel: calm, premium, curious—not chaotic.  
Then watch once **without picture**.  
Narration must carry the story alone (Voice Bible).  
Together they should feel inevitable.

## 2. Standard Video Timeline

Target runtime: **60 seconds** (acceptable **55–60**). Align cuts to Editorial spine + Voice emotional curve.

```text
0:00 ──────── Hook
0:03 ──────── Question / Setup
0:10 ──────── Explanation
0:35 ──────── Twist
0:48 ──────── Perspective Shift
0:57 ──────── CTA
1:00 ──────── End
```

| Window | Beat | Picture job | Audio job |
|--------|------|-------------|-----------|
| **0–3s** | **Hook** | One strong image or micro-motion that matches the opening line | Invitation energy (Voice: 2–3) |
| **3–10s** | **Question / Setup** | Establish phenomenon; light label if needed | Curiosity open |
| **10–35s** | **Explanation** | Clearest teaching visuals; diagrams if earned | Stable competence |
| **35–48s** | **Twist** | Visual reframe (model upgrade)—not a jump scare | Discovery weight |
| **48–57s** | **Perspective Shift** | Afterimage in ordinary life (bird, sky, blink, year) | Reflective; more space |
| **57–60s** | **CTA** | Quiet brand lockup / soft end card | Soft invite—never barked |

### Timing flexibility

| Situation | Allowed adjust |
|-----------|----------------|
| Dense physics | Explanation may run to ~0:38; compress twist slightly |
| Emotional finale (GS-008 / GS-010) | Give perspective **extra** 1–2s; trim explanation fat |
| Hook is a question | May bleed to 0:04—never past 0:05 without payoff starting |

### Hard rules

1. Do not put the visual climax in the first 3 seconds.  
2. Do not rush the ending to fit a loud CTA.  
3. Cut on meaning—not on every clause.  
4. If VO pause is reflective, **hold the picture**—don’t panic-cut.

## 3. Shot Design

### Duration

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| **Maximum shot duration** | **~6 seconds** before a meaningful change (move, cut, or graphic evolve) |
| **Average shot duration** | **~2.0–3.5 seconds** |
| **Hold for reflection** | Up to **~5–7 seconds** on perspective endings only |
| **Minimum shot** | Avoid sub-0.4s flash cuts except rare rhythmic teaching beats |

### Camera philosophy

| Move | Use when | Avoid when |
|------|----------|------------|
| **Static** | Diagrams, labels, integrity statements | Never as default laziness—static must feel composed |
| **Slow push-in (zoom)** | Curiosity deepen; approach a detail | Fast zooms, crash zooms, “impact” zooms |
| **Gentle pull-out** | Reveal context; cosmic humility | Constant breathing zoom on every shot |
| **Pan / tilt** | Follow a process (orbit, tear film, timeline) | Whip pans; fake handheld chaos |
| **Parallax / multiplane** | Premium depth on illustrations | Overused 3D swing |

### Visual breathing room

- Prefer **negative space** over filled frames.  
- Keep subject in the **upper-middle** safe zone for Shorts (leave room for captions).  
- One hero element; supporting elements at ≤30% visual weight.  
- After dense diagram beats, return to a simple image for 2–3s.

### Framing for 9:16

| Zone | Use |
|------|-----|
| Top ~12% | Avoid critical detail (platform UI / notches) |
| Center | Primary subject |
| Lower ~18–22% | Captions / lower-thirds only |
| Edges | Safe margin ≥ **5%** from all sides |

## 4. Visual Storytelling

Choose the **simplest medium that tells the truth**.

| Medium | Prefer when | Avoid when |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| **Icons** | Abstract relationships, quick myth marks | Decorating every sentence |
| **Illustrations** | Mechanisms (domains, blink, yawn) needing clarity | Pseudo-realistic anatomy that misleads |
| **Real footage** | Lived phenomena (sky, bird, paperclip, year montage) | Generic stock that could be any brand |
| **Motion graphics** | Processes, fields, timelines | Constant kinetic wallpaper |
| **Diagrams** | Causal chains, “known vs unknown” | Walls of boxes |
| **Maps** | Impact sites, Earth context (Chicxulub) | Spinning globes for no reason |
| **Microscope imagery** | Cellular / surface science (rare in Founding set) | Shock “gross-out” framing |
| **Space imagery** | Black holes, cosmic scale—prefer NASA/ESA-class | Fake neon accretion clickbait art |
| **Historical imagery** | Deep time / paleontology context | Sensational extinction firestorms |
| **Infographics** | Comparisons, sparse stats only if Editorial allows | Number spam |

### Category defaults (Founding Collection spirit)

| Category | Primary visual language |
|----------|-------------------------|
| Space | Space imagery + restrained diagrams |
| Physics | Illustration / motion graphics + real object (magnet, sky) |
| Biology / body | Soft real close-ups + simple mechanism art |
| Mind / psychology | Metaphoric clean imagery; avoid brain-porn clichés |
| History / deep time | Map + restrained archive + living afterimage (bird) |
| Technology | Abstract signal graphics + familiar device context |

### Integrity in picture

- **Established** → solid, clear visuals  
- **Hypothesis** → softer treatment; optional dashed / muted styling  
- **Unknown** → space, silence, incomplete graphic—not a fake answer animation

## 5. Visual Hierarchy

### On-screen text roles

| Role | When | Limit |
|------|------|-------|
| **Title / topic chip** | First 3–5s optional | ≤ **6 words** |
| **Labels** | Naming a structure once | ≤ **3 words** typical |
| **Annotations** | Point to a part | One at a time |
| **Callouts** | Myth correction (“Not gravity”) | Rare; calm |
| **Scientific notes** | Evidence layer tags if needed | “Established” / “Open question”—not paragraphs |
| **Comparison cards** | Side-by-side only when essential | Max **2** panels |

### Screen balance

```text
┌─────────────────────┐
│     (breathing)     │
│                     │
│     HERO IMAGE      │
│                     │
│  optional label     │
│                     │
│ ░░ caption band ░░  │
└─────────────────────┘
```

### Maximum text on screen

| Rule | Spec |
|------|------|
| Simultaneous text blocks | **1** primary (+ captions) |
| Characters per on-screen title | **≤ 42** |
| Lines of annotation | **≤ 2** |
| Full sentences on screen | Prefer **no**—VO carries sentences |
| Captions | See Typography (chapter 7) |

### Never

- Bullet lists mid-Short  
- Tiny footnotes viewers can’t read  
- Competing lower-third + big title + icon row

## 6. Color System

Align with Curionex product brand tokens where useful; video grades may be slightly softer than UI neon.

### Primary palette

| Role | Guidance | Reference (product) |
|------|----------|---------------------|
| **Backgrounds** | Deep neutral / soft dark / clean light—pick **one** world per video | Near-black / charcoal family |
| **Primary accent** | Warm brand amber–orange for focus marks | `--brand-amber` `#f0a020` · `--brand-orange` `#e8720c` |
| **Highlight yellow** | Sparse spark only | `--brand-yellow` `#f5c518` |
| **Foreground text** | High contrast white/off-white on dark, or near-black on light | — |

### Semantic colors (science UI)

| Meaning | Color behavior |
|---------|----------------|
| **Established / known** | Stable cool-neutral or soft teal; solid lines |
| **Hypothesis / leading idea** | Muted violet-gray or soft dashed amber—**never neon mystery** |
| **Unknown / open question** | Desaturated; incomplete shapes; more negative space |
| **Warning / myth correction** | Soft coral/rose—not alarm red strobe (`--danger` only for true error states in UI, not VO scare) |
| **Success / survive / living continuity** | Soft green used sparingly (e.g. bird survival)—never “stock market up” |

### Accessibility

- Contrast for captions ≥ WCAG **AA** against background plate.  
- Do not encode meaning by color alone—pair with label/shape.  
- Avoid pure red/green only encodings.  
- Test on phone outdoors (brightness).  

### Color discipline

Orange guides attention—**it should not flood every surface** (same rule as product UI).

## 7. Typography

### Font philosophy

| Layer | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **UI / studio** | Geist Sans / Geist Mono (product) |
| **Video titles & labels** | Clean grotesque sans; high legibility at small sizes |
| **Avoid** | Novelty fonts, sci-fi chrome, comic display, heavy serifs in Shorts UI |

### Hierarchy

| Level | Use | Size guide (1080 wide) |
|-------|-----|-------------------------|
| H1 moment | Rare topic lock | ~64–84 px |
| Label | Structure names | ~36–48 px |
| Caption | Full VO captions | ~42–54 px |
| Meta | Tiny codes only if needed | ≥ 28 px |

### Subtitle / caption rules

| Rule | Spec |
|------|------|
| Style | Burned-in or platform captions; **same wording as VO** |
| Reading speed | Sync to narration; **≤ ~2 lines** visible |
| Max characters per line | **~32–42** |
| Max lines | **2** (3 only in emergency) |
| Safe margin | ≥ **5%** sides; sit in lower third without covering mouth/subject |
| Emphasis | Bold sparingly for twist words—not every line |
| profanity / shock | N/A—Curionex doesn’t write that way |

### Reading & comprehension

If a viewer watches muted with captions only, the Editorial story must still work.  
Captions are accessibility + silent feed performance—not a second script.

## 8. Animation Principles

Motion **guides attention**. It never competes with VO.

| Technique | Use | Duration guide |
|-----------|-----|----------------|
| **Fade** | Scene change; reflective holds | 8–16 frames @ 30fps |
| **Scale** | Soft emphasis on a label/object | Subtle 100→105% max |
| **Slide** | Introduce diagram parts in order of explanation | One element at a time |
| **Highlight** | Trace a path (field lines, tear film) | Follow VO noun |
| **Diagram animation** | Build → hold → simplify | Build during explanation only |
| **Map animation** | Ease to location; hold | No hyper spins |
| **Timeline animation** | Left-to-right or deep-time descent—slow | Match “66 million” calm VO |
| **Physics animation** | Domains aligning, light scattering | Accurate > flashy |

### Easing

Prefer gentle ease-in-out.  
Avoid elastic / bounce / “game UI” overshoot on science.

### Never

- Continuous spin logos  
- Shake on every cut  
- Particle storms as wallpaper  
- Animating text faster than it can be read

## 9. B-Roll

### Preference ladder

1. **Purpose-shot or purpose-illustrated** for the script  
2. **Authoritative public science imagery** (NASA / ESA / ESO / USGS / museum)  
3. **Licensed premium stock** that could only fit this idea  
4. **3D renders** when real capture impossible (interiors of models)—label honesty if speculative  
5. **AI-generated visuals** — last resort; see rules below  

| Source | Prefer | Reject |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Stock footage** | Specific, quiet, high grade | Generic “wow science” packs |
| **Historical images** | Credited; tasteful | Sensational death tableaux |
| **NASA / ESA / ESO** | Cosmic & Earth science | Over-processed fake color without note |
| **Microscope** | True to scale context | Horror framing |
| **3D renders** | Clear mechanism models | Trailer black-hole porn |
| **Illustrations** | Custom to Curionex clarity | Clip-art clutter |
| **AI-generated** | Only if reviewed for artifacts & scientific honesty | Default pipeline; fake “photo” of real people/events |

### AI-generated visual rules

- No fabricated “real photos” of historical events.  
- No misleading anatomy.  
- Disclose in production notes when used.  
- Art-direct to match chapters 1 & 6—not Midjourney default glitter.  
- Editorial integrity > pretty.

### Credits

Keep a production credit list for licensed and agency imagery.  
On-screen credits only if legally required—prefer end-card micro or description.

## 10. Sound Design

Picture and VO lead. Sound design **supports**.

| Element | Guidance |
|---------|----------|
| **Background music** | Sparse, warm, low-competition bed—or **none** |
| **Silence** | Valid and premium—especially unknowns & endings |
| **Ambient** | Soft real-world beds (air, room, distant nature) at low level |
| **Transitions** | Prefer invisible cuts; rare soft whoosh only if it clarifies a move |
| **Whooshes / impacts** | Almost never; banned on every cut |
| **UI beeps** | Avoid |

### Music rules

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Duck under VO automatically | Let music fight consonants |
| Fade down before perspective lines | Climb to a trailer chorus at the twist |
| Match Voice emotional curve | Use TikTok trend tracks as identity |

### When NOT to use effects

- On every edit  
- Under integrity hedges  
- Under soft CTAs  
- To “sell” a myth correction  

> Callout: **If you can remove the SFX and the film gets more trustworthy, remove the SFX.**

## 11. CTA

### Curionex ending

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Soft verbal CTA from Master Script | “SMASH SUBSCRIBE” |
| Quiet brand mark / wordmark | Animated yelling end-card |
| Hold the perspective image | Hard cut to channel trailer |
| Leave 0.3–0.6s breath (Voice Bible) | Bark over the last idea |

### Visual CTA patterns (approved)

- Small wordmark fade in corner while afterimage holds  
- End card with brand + one-line promise (“Curiosity, clearly explained.”)—optional  
- No countdown, no bell animation, no multi-button spam  

### Brand-first · Curiosity-first

The CTA invites continued curiosity.  
It never punctures the perspective shift.

## 12. Editor Checklist

Before export, the editor confirms:

### Audio

- [ ] VO matches approved Master Script  
- [ ] Levels consistent; no clipping  
- [ ] Music ducked; SFX minimal  
- [ ] Ending reflective, not loud  

### Narration sync

- [ ] Cuts respect Voice pauses  
- [ ] Twist and perspective not visually rushed  
- [ ] Timeline windows roughly match chapter 2  

### Graphics

- [ ] One idea on screen  
- [ ] Labels accurate to Editorial layers  
- [ ] No junk text  
- [ ] Brand color discipline  

### Timing

- [ ] 55–60s final  
- [ ] Hook ≤ ~3–5s to promise  
- [ ] CTA ≤ last ~3s  

### Transitions

- [ ] Invisible or gentle  
- [ ] No whoosh spam  

### Accessibility

- [ ] Captions accurate & readable  
- [ ] Contrast OK  
- [ ] Safe margins  

### Scientific labels

- [ ] No fake certainty graphics  
- [ ] Unknowns not “solved” visually

## 13. Quality Levels

| Level | Meaning | Ship? |
|-------|---------|-------|
| **Bronze** | Rough assembly; timing only | Internal only |
| **Silver** | Clear picture + VO sync; temp graphics | Reviewable cut |
| **Gold** | Meets this Blueprint + Voice Bible + Editorial intent; publishable Founding-adjacent | **Yes** |
| **Platinum** | Reference craft; afterimage ending; motion invisible; could train freelancers | **Yes — highlight** |
| **Diamond** | Rare. Defines era of Curionex look; archival master; paired with Platinum script + Platinum VO | **Yes — landmark** |

### Gold minimum (picture)

- Timeline discipline  
- Breathing room  
- Accurate labels  
- No spectacle addiction  
- Soft CTA  
- Captions complete  

### Platinum signals

- Perspective image haunts after phone locks  
- Motion feels inevitable  
- Category-perfect visual language  
- Could mute-test as Curionex instantly  

### Diamond

Reserve for channel milestones—not weekly output.  
Requires director sign-off across Editorial + Voice + Picture.

## 14. Future AI Video

AI video tools (any provider) must **obey this Blueprint**—they do not invent a new aesthetic.

| Rule | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| Provider-neutral | Policy here; settings in studio/runbooks |
| Script-locked | Generate from approved Master Script + Story Spine only |
| Integrity | No fake evidence imagery |
| Motion | Prefer simple transforms; ban chaotic default templates |
| Look | Match color/type/hierarchy chapters |
| Human gate | No auto-publish without QA checklists |
| Voice sync | Respect Voice Bible pacing; don’t retime VO to fit flashy templates |

### Prompting AI video systems

Include in briefs:

1. “Wonder over spectacle; minimal; educational.”  
2. Timeline beat labels.  
3. Evidence layer for each graphic.  
4. “No trailer energy; no whoosh every cut.”  
5. Reference Platinum stills when available.

| Setting | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Resolution | **1080×1920** |
